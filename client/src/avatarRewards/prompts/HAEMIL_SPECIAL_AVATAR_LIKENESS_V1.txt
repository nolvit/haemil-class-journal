MODE GOAL — LIKENESS

“내 모습과 느낌을 최대한 그대로 유지한 스페셜 아바타.”

The result should clearly and immediately look like the same character as Image A.

IDENTITY PRIORITY

Identity preservation is the highest priority.

Preserve from Image A:
- overall facial identity
- face shape and facial proportions
- eye shape and eye impression
- eyebrow shape and character
- nose identity
- mouth and smile tendency
- hairstyle identity
- hair part, silhouette, and general flow
- glasses if they are part of the Master Avatar
- skin-tone impression
- age impression
- body proportion impression
- overall personal vibe

Do not reinterpret the student into a different attractive character.
Do not significantly change facial structure.
Do not make the eyes, jaw, nose, or lips dramatically different.

Light polishing is allowed only when it does not reduce recognizability.

OUTFIT APPLICATION

Apply the selected outfit references accurately:

Image B = Top
Image C = Bottom

Preserve the important properties of each selected item:
- color
- silhouette

- material impression

- overall styling mood

Do not replace a selected item with a random alternative.
Do not add unrelated outerwear.
Do not add obvious real-brand logos.

MOOD TAGS

Use the selected mood tags from ORDER DATA only as secondary styling guidance.

Mood tags may influence:
- expression
- pose
- hand gesture

- lighting nuance

Mood tags must NOT override identity preservation.

HAEMIL WORLD STYLE

Use the established Haemil World visual direction:
- premium polished semi-webtoon 2D illustration
- modern Korean student fashion
- high-quality webtoon character rendering
- natural anatomy
- clean linework
- refined textures
- age-appropriate presentation

- profile-quality finish suitable for sharing

Do not create photorealism.
Do not create a painted-over-photo appearance.
Do not use chibi proportions.
Do not use exaggerated anime facial distortion.

AGE AND BODY

Preserve the student’s Master Avatar age impression.
Do not age the character up.
Do not sexualize the character.
Do not make the physique unrealistically mature.

POSE AND COMPOSITION

Create a full-body or near full-body character image.
Keep the character as the clear focal point.

The 2 results must:
- show the same student identity
- use the same selected outfit combination
- use the same Haemil World art direction

The 2 results should differ mainly in:
- pose
- hand gesture
- facial expression
- subtle camera/composition nuance

Do not create a collage.
Do not combine the 2 results into one image.

BACKGROUND

Use a clean, simple, non-distracting background.
No UI.
No typography.
No busy environmental scene.

FINAL PRIORITY ORDER

1. Same Master Avatar identity
2. Accurate selected outfit
